Real-economy assets in the region are starved of institutional capital

Importers, exporters and mid-size companies in Central America and Mexico finance short-tenor, well-collateralized operations at bank speed and bank cost. Institutional investors, in turn, have few regulated ways to reach that paper.

$1.7Tglobal trade-finance gap (ADB, 2024)
70%of SME credit requests declined by traditional banks in the region
~$16Tof real-world assets expected to be tokenized by 2030 (BCG)

For issuers

Approvals of 30 to 45 days, high all-in cost and a single credit committee that can say no. Banks remain partners in this model: the marketplace adds a distribution channel, it does not replace them.

For capital providers

Access to USD-denominated, collateralized, short-duration assets from a growing region, with a regulated venue, standardized documentation and on-chain settlement instead of bilateral, paper-based deals.

A CNAD-licensed venue to issue and trade tokenized real-world assets

The Kabal Digital Marketplace (Kabal Invest) is operated by Kabal Bridge S.A. de C.V. under El Salvador's Digital Assets Issuance Law (LEAD) and PSAD licence PSAD-0056 (resolutions CNAD-023-2025, CNAD-CD-269-2025, CNAD-CD-274-2025).

Primary market

Issuers list security tokens after an admissions review, an independent certification of the asset and a Relevant Information Document (DIR) registered with CNAD.

Secondary market

Order book with price-time priority, delivery-versus-payment settlement on the Base network in USDC, bulletin board and OTC desk for block trades.

Rulebook

Published Listing Rules, Trading Rules, Investor Terms, a platform-level AML/KYC policy, a Conflicts of Interest policy and a Fee Schedule, all subject to CNAD oversight.

Kabal Bridge acts as structurer and operator; each issuance has its own issuer, collateral package and disclosure document.

How an issuance moves through the marketplace

1

Application and eligibility

Issuer application form, corporate and beneficial-ownership review, asset and token standards, self-certified document checklist.

2

Independent certification

An external certifier verifies existence, valuation, title, liens and insurance of the asset and produces the report annexed to the DIR.

3

DIR and CNAD registration

Relevant Information Document on the marketplace template; CNAD registration fee of 0.01% of the amount issued; typical review of 4 to 8 weeks.

4

Listing and subscription

KYC-whitelisted investors subscribe on the platform; tokens are ERC-7943 (uRWA) on Base, audited by Hacken.

5

Distributions and reporting

Monthly distributions in USDC to holder wallets, monthly reporting to token holders and CNAD, on-chain audit trail.

6

Secondary trading

Listed tokens trade on the venue with DvP settlement; liquidity partners may make markets or take blocks through the OTC desk.

Timelines are typical, not guaranteed; every issuance is subject to CNAD registration.

Investor protection is built into every listing

  • Independent external certifier on the asset before listing and on the collateral over the life of the token.
  • Collateral pledged to the vehicle: endorsed bills of lading, assigned invoices, property or custody receipts, never a promise.
  • Insurance and reserves where the asset calls for them: cargo and credit insurance on trade finance, reserve funds covering scheduled distributions, waterfall priority of payments.
  • Concentration limits per borrower, sector, carrier and geography, disclosed in each DIR.
  • KYC/AML at platform level, aligned with LEAD, CNAD regulations and FATF: PEP and sanctions screening, enhanced due diligence, transaction monitoring, reporting to the Financial Investigation Unit.
  • Conflicts of Interest policy covering the cases where Kabal acts as operator, issuer or liquidity provider: information barriers, recusal, disclosure to CNAD.
  • Investor Terms with explicit risk acknowledgements: capital at risk, liquidity, smart-contract and regulatory risk.
  • Monthly reporting to token holders and periodic reporting to CNAD; everything reconcilable on-chain.

Institutional stack, one chain

Base network

Coinbase Layer 2, the only chain of the Kabal ecosystem. Delivery-versus-payment settlement of listed tokens.

ERC-7943 (uRWA)

Compliance-aware token standard with transfer restrictions and whitelisting, co-developed by Brickken and Hacken.

Audit and custody

Smart contracts audited by Hacken. Custody with PrimeVault and Fireblocks; USDC settlement with Circle.

Platform and rails

Tokenization platform partner Brickken (ISO 27001). Electronic bills of lading via CargoX, WaveBL and Contour. US payment rails through BIVO Inc.

What this means for a liquidity partner: one onboarding (KYC) gives access to every issuance on the venue; positions are held in institutional custody; distributions and trades settle in USDC on a public, auditable ledger; documentation is standardized across issuers.

Four ways to provide liquidity

Anchor allocation

Commit to primary issuances that match your mandate (asset class, tenor, ticket, geography), with first look at the pipeline and standardized deal packages.

Programmatic facility

A credit line or warehouse facility to the originating vehicles (Kabal Capital, Kabal Trade Finance), with marketplace issuances as the take-out.

Private-placement note

The same collateral package in a note format, off-marketplace, for mandates that cannot hold tokens directly.

Secondary liquidity

Market-making or block trading of listed tokens through the order book, the bulletin board and the OTC desk.

Economic terms are agreed per mandate and shared under NDA. Participation is limited to qualified and institutional investors under the LEAD framework and subject to KYC/AML clearance.

Governance and compliance framework of the venue

Documents that govern the marketplace

  • Issuer Requirements and Checklist · Issuer Application Form
  • Listing Rules · Trading Rules
  • Issuer Agreement and Platform Terms · Investor Terms and Conditions
  • Relevant Information Document (DIR) template for every public issuance
  • AML/KYC Policy (platform level) · Conflicts of Interest Policy · Fee Schedule

Controls a partner can rely on

  • Market-integrity rules: prohibition of insider dealing, manipulation and wash trading; circuit breakers and trading halts.
  • Suspension and delisting powers of the operator; material-events notification by issuers.
  • Five-year record keeping, staff training and periodic policy review.
  • Disclosure of related-party issuances and proprietary positions.

Kabal Bridge is the structurer and operator; Kabal Capital is a non-bank lender in Honduras and is not supervised by the CNBS.
